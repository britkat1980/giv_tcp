Share your device

Help make GivTCP work with more GivEnergy devices. A capture records the Modbus data your inverter sends GivTCP for a few minutes, which can be added to GivTCP's library of real devices that every change is tested against.

  1. Click Capture for an inverter. It takes a few minutes, and GivTCP carries on as normal.
  2. When it's finished, click Download capture to save the file.
  3. Click Open GitHub issue. Your device details are filled in: drag the downloaded file into the issue and submit it. You'll need a free GitHub account.

Privacy: serial numbers and IP addresses are removed from the capture before it's saved, by the givenergy-modbus library GivTCP uses. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you attach the file to the issue yourself.
